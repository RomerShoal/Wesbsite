VERIFIED

Federal permitting bill — Senate vote 19 Nov

The Senate passed amended H.R. 2347, the Bipartisan American Affordability and Jobs Act (BAAJA), 62–33. It would change the historic-preservation Section 106 process, including consultation deadlines, and allow certain projects to continue during litigation while agencies address court-identified deficiencies. It now goes to the House; the timing is unclear. This matters because the rules and timing of historic-preservation review could change if the bill becomes law. APTA report, 24 Nov.
