What Transco says changed — 13 Nov

Transco describes the new filing as “essentially the same” as the 2025 application. Its revision table marks Cultural Resources “No revisions”; the entry for N.J.A.C. 7:7-9.34 identifies an update to the HPO concurrence date (2 July 2025). The offshore Raritan Bay Loop waterfront development plan remains listed as dated 8 October 2025. Transco lists changes to the construction timetable, Ambrose Channel drilling approach and dredging and disposal sections. It requests an NJDEP decision by 12 February 2027. Its modeling predicts an exceedance of New Jersey's human-health criterion for 4,4'-DDE at 500 feet; Transco argues the coastal rules permit temporary exceedances. These are statements from the applicant, not findings by NJDEP.

Federal permitting bill — Senate vote 19 Nov

The Senate passed amended H.R. 2347, the Bipartisan American Affordability and Jobs Act (BAAJA), 62–33. It would change the historic-preservation Section 106 process, including consultation deadlines, and allow certain projects to continue during litigation while agencies address court-identified deficiencies. It now goes to the House; the timing is unclear. This matters because the rules and timing of historic-preservation review could change if the bill becomes law. APTA report, 24 Nov.
